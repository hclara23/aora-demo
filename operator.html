What this figure does not include

These are vendor costs only. They exclude developer labour, payment fees and new features. Routine technical upkeep is included during the six-month engagement; afterwards Elevate’s security and store updates are the optional $200 a month above, and nothing obliges you to take them. Staff time for member support and for privacy and compliance administration is AORA’s own and is not counted here, because it is not a bill anyone sends you.

Designed around local usage

The launch estimate assumes about 1,000 users active in a month, around 100 daily users and up to 20 using the app at once. These are sizing assumptions to validate in launch testing, not a capacity guarantee. The growth table above is what happens as those numbers rise.

  • About 100 application requests, five routine emails and 50 MB of audio delivery per active user each month.
  • Modest primary database storage across both services, with backups, and content delivery sized to the audio library rather than to video.
  • English and Spanish content, El Paso local appointment times and US cloud hosting in Northern Virginia.
  • Breathing animations and generated audio run on the user’s device, keeping practice sessions light on server usage.

Email and push notifications carry the routine reminders, which is what keeps this budget where it is. Member support is handled by AORA’s own staff and is not a vendor line. Dedicated 24-hour support, clinical care, video streaming and AI services are outside this estimate.

Privacy and launch assurance

The budget allows for separated clinical and wellness services, encrypted storage, backups and recurring privacy administration. Clinical launch remains subject to completed risk assessment, applicable business associate agreements, access and recovery testing, and approved privacy procedures. This estimate does not establish HIPAA compliance or App Store approval. HHS cloud guidance.

How the estimate is calculated

Technology amounts are rounded monthly budget allowances, using 730 hosting hours in AWS US East (Northern Virginia). The baseline allows for five small application/job instances, two separately hosted databases with standby copies, two private outbound gateways and a shared traffic router. The allowance includes headroom; it is not an exact AWS calculator quote.

Direct sign-in for 1,000 users fits well within Cognito Essentials’ published 10,000-user free allowance, assuming that allowance is available to AORA. Additional identity features or enterprise sign-in may cost more. At SES’s published base rate, 20,000 outbound emails cost $2 before data and optional features. The delivery budget also covers app distribution. Public long-form video uses an external public video channel rather than this budget.
