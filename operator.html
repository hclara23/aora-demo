What to measure in a pilot

  • Time to complete a booking and proportion completed without staff help.
  • Staff minutes spent on booking and check-in, measured with consent in a small usability study.
  • Bookings recovered through the waitlist. The console counts retained bookings on that channel in the previous 30 days.
  • Failed payments and reconciliation exceptions, using existing operational records.
  • Total operating cost divided by active members, with maintenance included.

Do not collect clinical details, names or free-text session content in product analytics. There are no advertising pixels or third-party analytics SDKs in the authenticated experience.

The real cost model

The former $100–$115/month figure did not match the committed production configuration. Production defines two NAT gateways, Multi-AZ databases (small and micro, with 50 GB each), two wellness API tasks, one clinical API task and one worker task. The worker deployment must also be reconciled with the documented requirement for one isolated task per zone before release.

Networking, database storage and standby capacity, logs, backups, secrets, messaging, build services, payment processing, support and engineering time must all be accounted for.

Release gates

App Store approval and HIPAA readiness cannot be established by a demo. The operator must complete privacy notices, relevant business associate agreements, risk analysis, access reviews, restore drills, real integration tests and native device review before live use.

The repository release checklist records source links and the outstanding evidence. Apple requires accurate metadata, working backend services and reviewer access. Physical services use the applicable external-payment rules; digital goods need separate policy review.
