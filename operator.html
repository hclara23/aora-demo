Designed around local usage

The estimate assumes 5,000 users active in a month, around 500 daily users and up to 100 using the app at once. These are sizing assumptions to validate in launch testing, not a capacity guarantee.

  • Up to 1 million application requests, 20,000 emails and 2,000 US SMS segments per month.
  • 100 GB of primary database storage across both services, up to 100 GB of files and 100 GB of monthly file delivery.
  • English and Spanish content, El Paso local appointment times and US cloud hosting in Northern Virginia.
  • Breathing animations and generated audio run on the user’s device, keeping practice sessions light on server usage.

SMS is reserved for essential messages; longer or Unicode messages can use multiple billable segments. Routine support covers approximately 100 twelve-minute enquiries per month during scheduled hours. Dedicated 24-hour support, clinical care, video streaming and AI services are outside this estimate.

Services priced separately

Osmind clinical-record licensing and integration fees require a provider-specific quote. Initial development, migration, security assessment, legal review and native app submission are one-time launch costs scoped separately. Facility rent, equipment, clinical staff, marketing, business insurance and accounting belong in the centre’s operating budget.

Privacy and launch assurance

The budget allows for separated clinical and wellness services, encrypted storage, backups and recurring privacy administration. Clinical launch remains subject to completed risk assessment, applicable business associate agreements, access and recovery testing, and approved privacy procedures. This estimate does not establish HIPAA compliance or App Store approval. HHS cloud guidance.

How the estimate is calculated

Technology amounts are rounded monthly budget allowances, using 730 hosting hours in AWS US East (Northern Virginia). The baseline allows for five small application/job instances, two separately hosted databases with standby copies, two private outbound gateways and a shared traffic router. The allowance includes headroom; it is not an exact AWS calculator quote.

Direct sign-in for 5,000 users fits within Cognito Essentials’ published 10,000-user free allowance, assuming that allowance is available to AORA. Additional identity features or enterprise sign-in may cost more. At SES’s published base rate, 20,000 outbound emails cost $2 before data and optional features. The delivery budget also covers SMS and app distribution. Labour rates are budgeting assumptions, not local supplier quotes.
